LUMEN

Civic issue reporting — Android app

Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will warn that it came from outside the Play Store — choose Install anyway, or allow installs for your browser when prompted.
  3. Open LUMEN and sign in.

On iPhone, or prefer not to install anything? Use LUMEN in the browser — add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.
